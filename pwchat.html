Install Stuffchat

  1. Open this page in Safari (required — other browsers can't add to your home screen on iOS).
  2. Tap the Share icon (square with an arrow) in the toolbar.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add in the top-right corner.

Stuffchat will appear on your home screen with its own icon and open full-screen, with no address bar.

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share icon in the toolbar (top-right in most layouts).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

If you're in Split View, exit it first — the Share icon can be hidden in narrow layouts.

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app (wording varies by Chrome version).
  4. Confirm by tapping Add / Install.

Stuffchat opens in its own window afterward, separate from your browser tabs.

  1. Look for an install icon in the address bar (a small screen with an arrow), or open your browser's menu.
  2. Choose Install Stuffchat… (Chrome/Edge) or the equivalent option.
  3. Confirm the install prompt.

On browsers without app-install support (e.g. Firefox), bookmark this page instead for one-click access.
